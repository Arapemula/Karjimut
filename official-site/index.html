SUPERHUMAN AGENT WORKFLOW

Everything your AI agent needs to remember. Nothing it doesn't.

Built specifically for developers and Vibecoders who want superhuman coding speed without the headache of managing prompt files or fighting context bloat.

100% Autonomous Vibecoder Mode

Zero manual prompt engineering. Karjimut injects workspace rules so your agent calls its 3 mandates behind the scenes: loads architecture at start, notes milestones after coding, and pulls fixes on error.

The 5-Minute Sleep Cycle

When you step away for a coffee break, Karjimut wakes up. It distills chaotic episodic notes, throws out changelog noise, unlearns obsolete rules, and saves permanent architecture guidelines.

One-Shot Hybrid Bug Recall

Never let your agent repeat the same mistake. When a build or test fails, Hybrid FTS5 BM25 and 384-d semantic vectors (RRF) retrieve the exact fix with 100% Precision@1 and 0.0% false positives.

Always-On Elastic Core (Tier 1)

Auto-bootstraps from your project manifests (package.json, pyproject.toml, Cargo.toml) in <30ms. Guarantees your agent always knows your stack and rules without ballooning context.

Git-Native Team Memory

When you solve a tricky bug, Karjimut automatically exports it to `.karjimut/team_memory.json` with API-key redaction. When your teammate runs git pull, their AI agent inherits the solution.

100% Local-First & Private

All memory stays in local SQLite on your machine. Zero telemetry, zero cloud storage. Works completely offline with free local rule distillation or your choice of Gemini, Claude, Groq, or Ollama.
